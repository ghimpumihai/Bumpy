## Decision log

Decisions D1–D17 were researched and then reviewed by an independent advisor model before being accepted. D18 and D19 are scope cuts made by the team. D20 is a correctness fix found while rechecking the plan. D21–D31 move the backend from AWS to Azure (same day); D30 also fixes gaps found in a review of the AWS version (what starts the backfill, where aggregation runs, concurrent migrations). D32–D33 make the live path (phone → cloud → dashboard) the main path. D34–D36 come from the final research pass (02 §20). D38 builds the laptop fallback upload and the finalize job's Parquet output.

| D45 | Aggregation: the roughness fit includes only the vehicles linked to the car through segments both drove (directly or through a chain); any other vehicle type (a lone bike, `unknown`) has no identifiable effect, is left out, and its cells show their percentile; without such a link to the car, the percentile fallback stays. The session MSDV stays the whole trip | Accepted | Abandon the fit for everyone when one vehicle can't be identified (the previous behaviour); pick the reference vehicle from the data; weight sickness by whole pass duration | 02 §9, §10 |
| D46 | MSDV and roughness are computed from the fused `userAcceleration` projected on `gravity`, not from the raw accelerometer. CoreMotion's `gravity` always has length 1 g, so acceleration along it (vertical heave, what Wf weighs) can't be absorbed into it and stays in `userAcceleration`. A gravity estimate tilted by θ (sustained braking or cornering) puts an error of about θ·g into `userAcceleration`, almost all of it horizontal; the vertical part is g·(1 − cos θ) ≈ θ²·g/2, under 0.4 % of g for θ = 5°. The raw accelerometer (`accel_raw.csv`, batch `accelRaw`) is still recorded and kept to cross-check this after the real drive | Accepted | Raw accelerometer with our own gravity removal (a low-pass gravity estimate would itself eat the 0.1–0.5 Hz band MSDV needs) | 01 §3 M1/M2, 02 §9 step 6 |
| D47 | No passenger MISC ratings: the app no longer asks "how do you feel (0–10)" every 2 minutes, and the read API and dashboard show only the calculated dose (MSDV, % who may vomit), with no ratings and no rating–dose correlation. `sickness` labels from older app builds are still accepted and stored, but never returned | Accepted | Collect ratings and compare them with the dose (spec §3.7 H1) | 02 §9, §19 |
| D48 | No audio. No backend step ever used the sound level or the recordings (the surface classifier was never built), and recording a car or bus cabin is the project's biggest privacy cost. The backend drops it first: ingest no longer requires the batch's `audioLevel` or checks session.json `audioEnabled` (both are accepted and ignored while app builds that send them are still in use), the processor no longer counts or stores them, and the read API and dashboard no longer show them. The iOS app stops recording audio in its own PR. The `sessions.audio_enabled` column is left in place until then (dropping it while the old processor still writes it would break the rollout); `tools/upload` still skips `*.m4a`, which older session folders hold | Accepted | Keep the level meter for a later surface classifier | 01 §3.5, §6; 02 §10 |
| D49 | Side-to-side sway counts towards motion sickness: lateral acceleration (needs the learned forward axis) gets the Donohew & Griffin (2004) weighting (flat 0.0315–0.25 Hz, then −12 dB per octave; provisional Butterworth corners) and its dose adds to the ISO 2631-1 vertical one as energy, so MSDV = √(MSDV_z² + MSDV_y²) with k_y = 1. Every MSDV the system shows (live, series, session, segment a_w, MSI) is the combined one; `sessions.msdv_z` stays vertical and `msdv_y` holds the lateral part | Partly superseded by D50 (how Wy is built) | Vertical ISO only (D19); Wf on the lateral axis too; the whole horizontal plane (fore-aft included) | 02 §9 |
| D50 | The lateral weighting Wy is built to follow Donohew & Griffin's straight-line weighting within ±0.8 dB from 0.0315 to 0.8 Hz: a Butterworth high-pass at 0.0172 Hz (below the paper's range) and a second-order low-pass at the 0.25 Hz break with Q = 0.91 (fitted, minimax). The D49 Butterworth corners at 0.0315 and 0.25 Hz read 3 dB (29 %) low at both edges of the band the paper calls flat | Accepted | Butterworth corners at the band edges (D49); a least-squares fit (smaller average error, but up to 1.3 dB off) | 02 §9 |
| D51 | The phone app is a recorder only: Start (preflight runs silently, only a failing check shows, with its fix), Recording (timer, one status line, 2 s hold to end), Recap (data quality, share of the trip in the cloud), plus a menu panel (web app, Settings). No setup form (every trip is a car; mount and people keep the remembered defaults), no label buttons, no map or stream tables. Everything computed in the cloud (roughness, potholes, sickness) is shown on the web dashboard only. Light theme with the dashboard's neutral palette and an ink-black accent | Accepted | The operator dashboard with labels, map and per-stream table (01 §3.7, §7) | 01 §3.7, §7; `src/mobile-ios/README.md` |
| D52 | Dashboard redesign: the map is a heat map of trip overlap drawn from per-trip GPS lines (`GET /api/heat`) instead of coloured H3 cells; dark theme only; the Method and Quality pages, the per-seat sickness table and the vehicle/source badges are removed; Sessions lists trips and what they found; a floating dock (a menu button on phones) replaces the top bar | Accepted | Keep the coloured H3 cells; keep the light theme | 02 §5 |
| D53 | A session belongs to the device that sent it first: ingest records the first accepted batch's or `session.json`'s `deviceId` in `lake/owners/session=<sessionId>.json` (written once, never overwritten, cached per worker) and answers `403` to any later batch or `session.json` for that session from another `deviceId`, before anything is sent or archived. Without it, any valid key could post under another phone's sessionId and take its archive blobs first (the archive keeps the first copy). A marker that can't be written or read is a `503`, so the phone retries. Not mirrored in the mock (single key) | Accepted | Trust any key that may send as the body's `deviceId` (no owner); check the owner in the processor (too late: the archive blob is already taken) | 01 §6.2; `services/ingest/README.md` |
| D54 | A trip that gets new data after it ended (a backlog after the 15-minute close, a late Stop copy) goes back to `live`, not `processing`: it ends again by the usual rules (every batch in, or 15 quiet minutes) and is finalized once then. Finalize skips a stream message for a trip that is `live` again, and commits nothing if a batch or the Stop copy arrived while it ran. The processor ignores a batch or session.json whose deviceId isn't the session's device (outcome `foreign_device`: it can't turn a trip into a replay or add to it), and finalize skips such blobs in the raw archive | Accepted | Back to `processing` with an immediate resend (D37: a phone streaming on after the close started a finalize per batch); a time debounce in ready.py's sends | 02 §5; `services/processor/README.md` |
| D56 | The app records which parts of a trip the backend accepted (2xx/409) in `Documents/upload_acked/<sessionId>.txt`, and counts the 2 s windows that never entered the upload queue (upload off for part of the trip, a full disk) in the local `session.json` (`stats.uploadWindowsNotQueued`, omitted when 0 and stripped from the Stop copy, so the ingest contract is unchanged). The Recap's share counts every queued batch, every left-out window and the Stop copy, and only accepted parts count as sent; when a part will never arrive by itself it asks for the laptop upload. A trip may be deleted on the phone only when every batch and the Stop copy were accepted, nothing is queued or rejected and no window was left out. Settings shows "Retry failed uploads (N)" while `upload_failed/` holds files | Accepted | Percent of queued batches only (said "Complete" for a trip with gaps); a delete guard that only checks the queue and `upload_failed/` (let a trip recorded with upload off be deleted); laptop upload as the only way to re-send rejected parts | 01 §6.1; `src/mobile-ios/README.md` |
